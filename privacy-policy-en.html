Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

"Scroll Assist" (hereinafter referred to as "this app") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

1. Use of AccessibilityService API

This app utilizes Android's AccessibilityService API to perform gesture assistance for one-handed operation on large-screen smartphones.

  • Purpose of Use: The AccessibilityService API is used strictly to execute system-level actions (such as scrolling up/down, page jumping, and navigating back) on behalf of the user when interacting with screen target areas.
  • No Data Collection or Transmission: We DO NOT collect, store, record, or transmit any sensitive information, user inputs, on-screen text, or personal data via the AccessibilityService API. All operations are processed locally on your device.

2. Data Collection and Third-Party Sharing

We do not collect, retain, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, device identifiers, or location data. Furthermore, no user data is shared with third parties.

3. Local Data Storage

All application configurations (such as target area positions, sizes, and assigned features) are stored locally on your device using DataStore / Preferences and are never uploaded to external servers.

4. Regulatory Compliance (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)

Because this app does not collect, process, or transmit any personal data, no personal data processing subject to regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) takes place.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
